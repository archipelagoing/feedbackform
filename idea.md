## FeedbackForm — Collaborative Ideation & Consensus Platform

**Project Type:** Agentic AI / Collaborative Decision-Making / Natural Language Processing

### Project Idea

FeedbackForm is an AI-powered collaborative ideation platform that helps groups turn individual ideas, preferences, and feedback into a shared proposal.

Users can submit ideas, upload documents, highlight specific passages, and leave comments in plain English explaining what they like, dislike, want to preserve, or would change.

Rather than simply summarizing feedback or taking a majority vote, FeedbackForm uses agentic AI to interpret each participant's preferences, identify common ground, recognize conflicting requirements, and generate new proposals that incorporate the group's collective priorities.

The goal is to help teams move from **brainstorming → feedback → synthesis → consensus**, without losing the individual reasoning behind each contribution.

### Core Features

1. **Collaborative Annotation:** Users highlight and comment on specific portions of submitted ideas or documents.
2. **Natural Language Preference Extraction:** AI interprets feedback into structured preferences, requirements, objections, and suggestions.
3. **Agreement & Conflict Detection:** Agents identify overlapping interests, disagreements, and potentially compatible ideas.
4. **Agentic Idea Synthesis:** AI generates new proposals by combining promising elements from multiple ideas while respecting group constraints.
5. **Explainable Recommendations:** Each synthesized proposal traces its features back to the original ideas and participant feedback.
6. **Iterative Consensus Building:** Participants review AI-generated proposals, provide additional feedback, and repeat the process until an acceptable direction emerges.

### Agentic AI Components

- **Preference Analysis Agent:** Understands and structures individual feedback.
- **Consensus Analysis Agent:** Identifies agreement, disagreement, and trade-offs.
- **Idea Synthesis Agent:** Generates and refines proposals based on collective preferences.
- **Evaluation Agent:** Assesses preference coverage, constraint satisfaction, and unresolved objections.

### Example Use Case

Four graduate students are deciding on an AI/ML project. Each has different interests: autonomous planning, code execution, machine learning, and multi-agent coordination.

Instead of debating entire proposals, they highlight the specific features they value across several project ideas.

FeedbackForm analyzes their annotations and proposes a new project that combines compatible features, explains which preferences it satisfies, and highlights any remaining disagreements for discussion.

### Technical Focus

The project emphasizes multi-agent orchestration, natural language understanding, semantic similarity, preference modeling, constraint-aware generation, iterative reasoning, and human-in-the-loop evaluation.

**Primary Objective:** Build an intelligent system that helps groups discover and develop ideas they can collectively support, while preserving individual preferences, making trade-offs transparent, and keeping final decisions in human hands.
